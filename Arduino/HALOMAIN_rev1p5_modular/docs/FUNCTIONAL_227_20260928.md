Diagnostic-triggered production UI handlers are distinguished from physical
hit-testing, encoder motion and visual inspection. A fixed actuator cannot
select arbitrary Settings or shopping-list positions. Voice semantics require
a known acoustic phrase and matched list change; ambient recording alone only
tests transport. Test-item deletion must identify a newly created test record,
never choose a pre-existing user item arbitrarily.

No provisioning reset, credential change, factory flash, public release,
artificial OTA allowance or schedule change belongs to this campaign. New OTA
transfer, physical power interruption and full phone provisioning therefore
remain separate acceptance areas. An existing no-update check against older
published 224 is specifically a downgrade-rejection test, not equal-version
or transfer acceptance.

Reuse existing bounded controllers with reviewed 227 build/slot bindings.
